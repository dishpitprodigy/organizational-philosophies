# Prototype Notes

## Question

Can a branching, fact-based intake form make the boundary between service work, Assisted Intake, sponsorship-required work, and a reviewable Work Proposal understandable enough to demonstrate—while also showing how one submission could generate stakeholders, review modules, delivery sizing, risk indicators, and a hypothetical ticket structure?

## Variants to evaluate

- **A — Guided interview:** Does progressive disclosure help requesters answer unfamiliar questions without feeling buried?
- **B — Proposal worksheet:** Is showing the entire evidence record at once better for experienced requesters and reviewers?
- **C — Routing conversation:** Does an interview-like presentation and visible routing map make the organizational workflow easier to explain to an audience?

## Verdict

Not decided. Record which interaction model worked, what should be combined, and what the decision tree failed to ask before this prototype is deleted or rewritten.
